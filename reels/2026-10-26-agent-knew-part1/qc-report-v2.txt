(2) PER-SENTENCE WPM — METHOD
 Sentence boundaries from ffmpeg silencedetect (noise=-40dB, d=0.15) on the
 single-pass file. 14 silences detected for 8 sentence boundaries + 5 intra-sentence
 comma pauses + trailing silence. Boundaries were snapped to the nearest sentence-end
 silence and cross-checked two ways: (a) each boundary sits within 0.5s of the
 cumulative duration predicted from same-speed diagnostic renders, and (b) every
 intra-sentence pause in the single-pass file lines up with the same pause in the
 diagnostic render of that sentence (e.g. s5 comma pause, s7 comma pause, s9 comma
 pauses all match to ~0.15s). Diagnostic per-sentence renders at the SAME fixed
 speed 100 were used only as a measurement cross-check, never spliced in.
 Segment = caption span (speech start to next speech start, i.e. including the
 inter-sentence pause, matching how captions.ass and the beat cuts are built).
 wpm = words / segment seconds * 60. Average = mean of the 9 sentence wpms.

 WHY THE PACE GATE CANNOT BE MET (measured, not assumed)
 - The slow tokens ARE the mandated content: s8's "retroactive permission
   laundering" (10 syllables in 3 words) and s9's digits ("1,081" spoken as
   "one thousand eighty-one", "70%"/"8%" as "seventy/eight percent") run at
   ~110-135 wpm at fixed speed 100. Raising s9 to the band floor needs ~10-15
   added fast words — i.e. inventing filler — and removing words cannot help
   (cutting fast words leaves the rate unchanged; cutting the slow words would
   delete the facts). Same for s8 (~10 added words needed).
 - Render-to-render variance exceeds the band: IDENTICAL unchanged sentences
   measured 170.45 -> 177.11 -> 181.82 wpm (s3), 171.18 -> 176.34 -> 181.68 (s4),
   156.41 -> 163.31 -> 159.83 (s6), and s2 (comma->dash only) 190.72 -> 167.69
   -> 188.38 across the three full renders. The +/-8% band is tighter than the
   engine's own variance at fixed speed 100.
 - The pace fixes and the duration cap CONFLICT: every pace fix (added words /
   pauses) lengthens the video further past 40s (R1 +1.288s, R2 +3.088s over).
   The trims that would bring R1/R2 under 40s are exactly the added words
   (e.g. R1: cut "in the paper's mock tests", ~1.2s) — i.e. undoing the pace
   rewrites. The only version satisfying the duration cap is the verbatim v0.
