## What changed vs Vishnu's original script (and why)

Vishnu's original Tuesday script was ~100 words / ~45–55s of voiceover. The template
requires 80–90 words / 36–40s. Changes made in this draft:

1. **Hook kept, trimmed by one word.** Original: "You don't have an email problem.
   You have a blank-page problem." (11 words) → "You don't have an email problem —
   just a blank page." (10 words). Same idea, fits the 8–10 budget.
2. **"How long does one email take you? Ten minutes? Fifteen?" cut.** It was a
   rhetorical opener that cost 10 words and duplicated the hook's job.
3. **"Thirty seconds instead of fifteen minutes" cut from the voiceover.**
   It is rhetorical framing, not a Google product fact (see fact-check.md). It is
   NOT deleted from the project — it is flagged for Vishnu's call: restore it only
   as his own measured experience, never as a Google claim.
4. **"The trick is you stay in charge — the AI drafts, you decide" moved.**
   The line survives as the Feature 3 on-screen overlay ("AI drafts. You decide.")
   and as the spoken Feature 3 beat in his own words ("You read it once, fix
   what's off, then send"). The exact original sentence did not fit the beat budget.
5. **CTA changed to the format CTA.** Original: "Save this for the next email
   you're dreading." → "Follow for a new AI tool every day." The template mandates
   the follow CTA on tool reels. The save prompt can live in the description if
   Vishnu wants it back.
6. **"Try it on the next email you're dreading. You'll never go back to the blank
   page." cut.** Closing encouragement, cost 15 words, duplicated the CTA's job.
