## What is NOT claimed anywhere

- No time-savings number (no "saves X minutes", no "30 seconds").
- No price ($19.99/mo Pro is third-party only, unused).
- No claim that it is free for everyone, everywhere (it is free for personal
  accounts in the US; Workspace users need an eligible plan; personal accounts
  outside the US need a paid Google AI plan).
- No claim that it "writes perfect emails" or replaces checking (Google's own
  page says it can be inaccurate — the reel says you stay in charge).
