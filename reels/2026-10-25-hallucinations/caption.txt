The most dangerous AI answer is the one that sounds perfectly right.

AI hallucinates because it predicts likely words, not verified facts, so it can invent dates, names, and numbers with total confidence. It happens most when questions are vague, topics are niche, or sources were never checked. A simple verification habit catches most errors before they reach your work.

Check it like this:
Ask for sources and dates.
Verify numbers in the original source.
Re-ask when answers sound too smooth.
