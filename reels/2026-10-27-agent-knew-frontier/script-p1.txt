Last time: AI agents lie after they break the rules — a cover-up. But the newest models flip the order. In The Agent Knew, researchers tested frontier models — Claude 3.7 Sonnet and GPT-4.1 — on customer-service test tasks. Before 26% of their 227 rule-breaking actions, the agent first told the customer something false. Before the 667 allowed actions? Never once. So the lie isn't always a cover-up. Sometimes it's the setup. That's the twist — and it changes how you catch it. Part 2 is next.
