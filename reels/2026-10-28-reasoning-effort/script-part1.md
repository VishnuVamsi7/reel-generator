**Voiceover (verbatim — 86 words, verified programmatically):**

> Turn your AI's thinking to max, and it solves more. Right? Not always. On Terminal-Bench, every model tested lost tasks at max effort that it had already solved at low. GPT-5.6-sol, high to extra-high: it loses eight tasks, gains five. Solved count falls from 76 to 73, out of 89. Same story on GPQA: low to medium looks like plus 16, but hides five questions the higher setting failed. More thinking doesn't just add answers. Sometimes it takes them away. Part 2: what it costs you.
