## Muted-test verdict

- **All 3 beats PASS muted.** No beat is carried by audio alone: each beat's story (warped by the funnel / approved despite gaps / fixed filter returns the full answer with gaps caught aside) is visible in the images + camera move + designed overlay with captions off.
- **Beat redesigned for the muted test — Beat 2.** An early tight push-in on the warped panel alone was rejected during drafting: it would crop the approval marker out of the end frame, and without the marker the beat reads as "warped panel in a gate" with no approval story — a muted failure. The shipped Beat 2 is a push-in centred slightly right that keeps the panel's gaps AND the glowing marker together in the end frame. No other beat failed the muted test; Beats 1 and 3 had their end frames framed from the start to keep the story-critical detail in view (funnel edge in Beat 1, set-aside pieces in Beat 3).

## Why no Kit (written justification)

Kit is not in this build, following the Day 1 Memory Tax precedent (also image-led, no Kit). A foreground character would cover the gaps, the tilted block, the gate's approval marker and the set-aside pieces — the exact details the muted test depends on — and would compete with figures that already fill the frame and carry blank top/bottom overlay zones. The supplied figures are the host; the camera move is the host motion. The character track on the canvas is therefore marked "none (image-led build)" on every beat rather than promising a host the video does not contain.
