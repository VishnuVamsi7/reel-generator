A tool can work and still mislead. Its printout said 'found 20 documents' — only the first 20 of 4,772 matches. The checker saw just the printout, so it approved a wrong answer.

In one fixed-answer test, rewriting the printout to state its limits, caps, and how to handle them flipped all 90 verdicts from accept to reject: 30 of 30 in scope, completeness, and uncertainty.

Honest limit: identifier errors barely moved, 18/30 to 14/30, and overclaim stayed at 94%. Clearer wording helps; it does not fix everything.
